<text>
**Design Movement:** Dark Masculine Minimalism — inspired by high-end men's wellness brands and premium coaching platforms (think Whoop, Headspace for Men, Arete Syndicate)

**Core Principles:**
- Deep black/charcoal backgrounds with warm amber/gold accents — authority without aggression
- Generous whitespace to let copy breathe — the words do the heavy lifting
- Strong typographic hierarchy using a serif display font (Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond) for headlines and a clean sans-serif (DM Sans) for body
- Photography-forward — Shaun's professional photos are the hero, not decorative

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: Near-black charcoal (#0D0D0D / oklch(0.1 0.005 285))
- Accent: Warm amber/gold (#C9A84C / oklch(0.72 0.12 75)) — represents fire, transformation, earned excellence
- Text: Off-white (#F5F0E8) — warm, not clinical
- Secondary: Deep forest green (#1A3A2A) — grounded, natural, nervous system

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Asymmetric editorial layout — text left, image right on hero; alternating on subsequent sections
- Full-bleed sections with strong vertical rhythm
- Sections separated by thin amber rule lines, not dividers

**Signature Elements:**
- Thin horizontal amber line as a recurring motif (like a pulse line or horizon)
- Large oversized section numbers (01, 02, 03) in muted gold — editorial feel
- Pull quotes in large serif italic — philosophical disruption moments

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Scroll-triggered fade-ins — content reveals as you descend, like uncovering truth
- Hover states on CTAs use amber glow, not just color change

**Animation:**
- Subtle parallax on hero image
- Text lines fade in from bottom on scroll
- CTA buttons pulse gently on hover

**Typography System:**
- Display: Cormorant Garamond Bold — ancient, authoritative, unforgettable
- Body: DM Sans Regular — clean, modern, accessible
- Accent: Cormorant Garamond Italic — for pull quotes and philosophical statements

<text>
**Design Movement:** Raw Editorial Brutalism — inspired by independent men's magazines (Kinfolk, Monocle) and premium personal brand sites

**Core Principles:**
- Off-white/cream paper-like background with stark black type — honest, no-frills authority
- Oversized typography as the primary design element
- Heavy use of contrast — black blocks against cream, full-bleed dark sections for CTAs
- Photography treated as editorial spreads, not hero banners

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: Warm cream (#F7F3EC)
- Primary text: Near-black (#1A1A1A)
- Accent: Burnt sienna/terracotta (#C4622D) — warmth, fire, masculine energy
- Dark sections: Deep charcoal (#1C1C1C)

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Magazine grid — two and three column layouts that break into full-bleed moments
- Section headlines run at 90 degrees vertically on the left margin
- No rounded corners — everything is sharp and intentional

**Signature Elements:**
- Oversized running text (e.g., "UNFORGETTABLE" spanning full width)
- Thick black horizontal rules between sections
- Photography in stark black and white with terracotta color overlays

**Typography System:**
- Display: Bebas Neue — bold, all-caps, commanding
- Body: Libre Baskerville — editorial, trustworthy
- Accent: Libre Baskerville Italic — for quotes

## CHOSEN DESIGN: Response 1 — Dark Masculine Minimalism

**Rationale:** This is the closest to the Arete Syndicate aesthetic Shaun referenced — dark, authoritative, premium — but with a warmer, more human quality through the amber accents and serif typography. The Cormorant Garamond + DM Sans pairing gives it philosophical depth without being cold. The asymmetric editorial layout avoids the "AI slop" centered-everything trap.
